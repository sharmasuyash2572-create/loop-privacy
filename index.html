Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Loop ("we", "our", or "us") is a UPI money tracking app for Android. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it.

1. What we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number (entered by you)
  • Transaction data: merchant name, amount, date and category
  • App preferences and settings

We do not collect:

  • Raw SMS message content
  • OTPs or authentication codes
  • Personal or non-financial messages
  • Bank account numbers or card numbers
  • Location data

2. SMS permission

Loop requests permission to read SMS messages on your device. This permission is used exclusively to detect UPI transaction alerts sent by Indian banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, and others).

SMS messages are processed entirely on your device. Raw SMS content is never transmitted to our servers, stored in any external database, or shared with any third party. Only the extracted transaction data (amount, merchant name, date) is saved locally on your device.

We do not read personal messages, OTPs, promotional messages, or any non-financial SMS.

3. Gmail permission

If SMS permission is unavailable, Loop may request read-only access to your Gmail account to detect bank transaction emails as a backup. We only access emails from known bank sender domains. We do not read, store, or transmit personal emails.

4. How we use your data

  • To display your transaction history and spending analytics
  • To categorise transactions automatically
  • To calculate your savings rate and financial metrics
  • To personalise the app experience with your name and preferences

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to any third party.

5. Data storage

All your financial data is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. We do not operate servers that store your transaction history or personal financial information.

6. Third-party services

Loop uses Google Sign-In for optional account creation. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data processed through their service. We use no advertising SDKs or analytics platforms that share your data with third parties.

7. Your rights

  • Access: View all your data inside the app
  • Correction: Edit your profile at any time
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data from Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  • Revoke permissions: Remove SMS or Gmail access from Android Settings → Apps → Loop → Permissions at any time

8. Children's privacy

Loop is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
